These terms govern the booking of the tours and experiences offered by the
agency. By booking, the traveler confirms they have read and accepted them. The
agency may update them at any time; the version in force is the one published on
this page at the time of booking.

## Bookings

- A booking is confirmed once payment is recorded (in full or the required
  minimum deposit) and the agency sends the confirmation with the booking
  number.
- The traveler is responsible for the accuracy of the information provided —full
  name, ID document, phone and email. Incorrect details may prevent boarding or
  invalidate insurance coverage.
- Places are limited and assigned in order of confirmed payment.
- The agency may require a minimum number of participants to operate a
  departure. If it is not reached, the section «Changes and cancellations by the
  agency» applies.

## Payments and deposits

- Prices are published per person and include only what the tour description
  states. Anything not listed is at the traveler's expense.
- Full payment or an initial deposit is accepted when the tour allows it. The
  balance must be settled before the departure date, within the term informed by
  the agency.
- Payments are processed through the enabled payment gateway. The agency does not
  store full card details.
- An unpaid balance past the deadline allows the agency to release the place,
  applying the same cancellation rules as if the traveler had withdrawn.

## Cancellations by the traveler

Unless the tour description states otherwise, these terms apply, counted from the
departure time:

- **More than 15 days before:** 100% refund of the amount paid, less the payment
  gateway transaction fees.
- **Between 15 and 7 days before:** 50% refund of the amount paid.
- **Less than 7 days before, or no-show:** no refund.

Cancellation requests must be made in writing through the published contact
channels. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and may take up to 30
business days, depending on the issuing bank.

When possible, the traveler may request a date change or transfer the place to
another person instead of a refund, subject to availability and any fare
difference.

## Changes and cancellations by the agency

- The agency may modify itineraries, schedules or routes for weather, public
  order, safety or force majeure reasons, aiming to keep the experience
  equivalent.
- If the agency cancels a departure for not reaching the minimum number of
  participants, or for reasons attributable to it, the traveler may choose
  between rescheduling at no cost or a 100% refund of the amount paid.
- Force majeure and unforeseeable events —natural phenomena, road closures,
  decisions by the authorities— do not give rise to compensation beyond the
  refund or the rescheduling.

## Traveler responsibilities

- Arrive at the meeting point at the stated time, with a valid ID document and
  the gear recommended for the activity.
- Disclose, before booking, any medical condition, allergy, physical limitation
  or treatment that may affect participation.
- Follow the guide's instructions and the rules of the areas visited. The agency
  may remove from the activity, with no refund, anyone who endangers their own
  safety or the group's, or who is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Minors travel accompanied by a responsible adult and with the corresponding
  legal authorization.
- Cover any damage caused to equipment, vehicles or third-party facilities during
  the activity.

## Agency responsibilities

- Deliver the services described in the tour with qualified staff and equipment
  in suitable condition.
- Inform in a timely manner about any relevant change to the itinerary.
- Hold the insurance required by the regulations applicable to the activity.
  Coverage is that of the contracted policy; travelers are encouraged to take out
  additional assistance insurance if they consider it necessary.
- The agency is not liable for lost or forgotten personal belongings, for costs
  arising from the traveler's lateness, or for services the traveler contracts
  directly with third parties.

## Images and content

Photographs and videos may be taken during the activity for promotional
purposes. Travelers who prefer not to appear may state so in writing before
departure and the agency will respect that decision.

## Personal data

The agency processes the traveler's personal data under Colombian Law 1581 of
2012 and its implementing decrees, for the following purposes: managing the
booking and the payment, communicating operational information about the
departure, meeting legal requirements and, if the traveler consents, sending
commercial information.

Data is kept for as long as the contractual relationship lasts and for the term
required by law. Travelers may access, update, correct or delete their data and
withdraw their consent by writing to the contact channels published by the
agency.

## Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by Colombian law. The parties will seek to resolve any
disagreement directly before turning to the ordinary courts or to consumer
protection mechanisms.
